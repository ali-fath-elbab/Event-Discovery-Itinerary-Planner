Venue Policies & Admission Guidelines

Please review the following organizer requirements prior to arriving at Teatro 90.

block

No Refund or Exchange Policy

Tickets are strictly non-refundable and cannot be rescheduled once purchased, except in the rare event of official show cancellation by the organizer.

qr_code_scanner

Digital QR Code Check-in

Each attendee must present their unique e-ticket with readable QR code directly from their phone screen. Printed physical vouchers are also accepted.

no_photography

Flash Photography & Audio Recording

Professional DSLR cameras and continuous video recording are strictly prohibited during the performance to respect the performers' original material.

schedule

Late Comers Policy

Doors shut promptly at 08:00 PM. Late arrivals will be held in the outer lobby until a suitable comedic intermission to avoid disrupting live recording.
